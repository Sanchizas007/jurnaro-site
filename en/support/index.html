Common questions

How do I log a trade?

Tap the plus button. Quick mode needs a symbol, a side and a result. Full mode adds the entry and exit, fills, fees, stop and target, tags, notes and chart pictures; the profit and the R are calculated for you.

How do I bring in trades from my broker or platform?

Export a history or statement as a CSV file from your broker or platform, then open Tools and Import trades. Jurnaro finds the columns, the date format and the number format itself and shows a preview before it adds anything. Trades already in the journal are not added twice.

Does Jurnaro connect to my broker?

No. It never asks for a broker login. You import files, or enter trades by hand.

Why does a profit differ from my broker's statement?

Fees, swaps and currency conversion differ between brokers. Open the trade and enter the profit and the fees exactly as the statement shows them; Jurnaro treats what you enter as the truth.

How do I back up, or move to a new phone?

Open Tools, then Backup and export, and choose a folder in Files or iCloud Drive. Jurnaro copies your journal there after changes. On the new phone, install Jurnaro and restore from the same folder.

How do I cancel the subscription?

In the app, open Settings and Manage subscription, or open Settings on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period.

I subscribed but the app asks me again.

Open the paywall and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I delete my data?

Delete the app, or delete an account with its trades inside Settings. Everything is stored on your device, so there is nothing for us to delete.
