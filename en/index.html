What it does

  • Logs a trade in ten seconds. Quick mode: symbol, side and result. Full mode: fills, scaling in and out, fees, stop and target, tags, notes and chart pictures.
  • Imports from any broker. Bring a CSV from your broker or platform; Jurnaro finds the columns, dates and numbers itself. No account linking, no passwords.
  • Shows your edge. Win rate, profit factor, expectancy, R-multiples, equity curve and drawdown, and results by weekday, hour, symbol, setup and mistake.
  • Keeps you inside your rules. Daily loss, maximum drawdown, profit target and trades-per-day limits, the way prop-firm challenges set them, with a clear warning before you cross one.
  • Calculates. Position size, risk/reward, pip value, expectancy, drawdown recovery and compounding, inside the app and free on this site.
  • Stays private. No account, no ads, no tracking. Back up to a folder you choose, lock it with Face ID, export as JSON, CSV or PDF.

Free calculators

Six calculators that run in your browser, with no sign-up:

  • Position size calculatorWork out how many lots or shares to trade so that a stop-out costs the percentage of your account you chose.
  • Risk/reward calculatorCompare what you risk with what you can win, and see the win rate at which the trade breaks even.
  • Pip value calculatorFind out what one pip is worth for the lots you trade, in the quote currency of the pair.
  • Expectancy calculatorTurn your win rate, average win and average loss into the average result per trade, in money and in R.
  • Drawdown recovery calculatorSee how much you must gain to recover a loss, and why deep drawdowns are so hard to climb out of.
  • Compounding calculatorSee what a steady monthly gain, with or without deposits, grows to over time.

What it is not

Jurnaro is a journal. It does not connect to brokers, place trades, send signals or give investment advice, and it holds no money. Trading carries risk of loss. Jurnaro is independent and is not affiliated with any broker, exchange, prop firm or trading platform.
