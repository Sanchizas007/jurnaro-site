Privacy policy

Jurnaro keeps your journal on your phone. There is no account.

What is stored, and where

Everything you enter — trades, notes, chart pictures, accounts, rules, playbooks and settings — is stored in a database and in files on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else, and we run no server that could receive it.

Backups

If you choose a backup folder (for example in Files or iCloud Drive), Jurnaro copies your journal there. The folder is yours; if it is in iCloud, Apple handles it under Apple's terms. We never see it. Your iPhone's own backup is also Apple's.

What is not collected

No name, email address, phone number, contacts or location. No broker login or account number: Jurnaro does not connect to brokers, and imports are read from a file on your device. No analytics or advertising software, no ads, and no tracking across other companies' apps or websites. The app does not ask for permission to track.

Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple. To see which products are bought and how many subscribers stay, Jurnaro tells RevenueCat (revenuecat.com) about purchases: an anonymous app user ID, the product, the purchase and renewal dates and the country of the App Store. It contains nothing from your journal and no name or email address. RevenueCat's own policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Apple Ads attribution

The app can ask Apple for an anonymous attribution token and pass it to RevenueCat, so that we can tell whether an installation came from one of our Apple Ads campaigns. The token does not identify you, is not linked to your journal, and is not used to track you. The advertising identifier (IDFA) is not read.

Permissions

Face ID: optional lock; the system checks your face and Jurnaro never receives biometric data. Notifications: optional reminders, scheduled on your device. Photos: you pick the pictures you add to a trade with the system picker; Jurnaro cannot see the rest of your library.

Your control

You can export everything as JSON, CSV or PDF, and delete everything by deleting the app. You can manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple Account settings at any time.

Children

Jurnaro is a tool for people who trade. It is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the bottom of the page changes with it. If you have a question, write to the address below.
