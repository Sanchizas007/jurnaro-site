Pip value calculator

Enter the number of lots, the contract size and the size of one pip. The calculator gives the value of one pip.

Pip value—

How it works

Pip value = lots × contract size × pip size, in the quote currency of the pair. Most pairs use a pip of 0.0001; pairs quoted in yen use 0.01. To get the value in your account currency, divide by the price of your account currency in the quote currency.

These calculators do arithmetic on the numbers you enter. They are not investment advice. Check the result against your broker's contract specifications before you trade.
