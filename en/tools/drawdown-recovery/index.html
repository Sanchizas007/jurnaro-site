How it works

Gain needed = drawdown ÷ (100 − drawdown). A 10% loss needs 11.1% to recover; 50% needs 100%; 80% needs 400%. This is why limiting the size of a loss matters more than the size of a win.

These calculators do arithmetic on the numbers you enter. They are not investment advice. Check the result against your broker's contract specifications before you trade.
