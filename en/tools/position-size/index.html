How it works

Size = money at risk ÷ (distance from entry to stop × value of a 1.0 move per lot). For a standard forex lot the value of a 1.0 move is 100,000 in the quote currency; for one share it is 1. Always check the contract size at your broker.

These calculators do arithmetic on the numbers you enter. They are not investment advice. Check the result against your broker's contract specifications before you trade.

Keep the numbers in a journal
