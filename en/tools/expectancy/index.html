How it works

Expectancy = win rate × average win − loss rate × average loss. In R, divide by the average loss. Profit factor = gross profit ÷ gross loss. A positive expectancy over many trades is the definition of an edge; a few trades prove nothing.

These calculators do arithmetic on the numbers you enter. They are not investment advice. Check the result against your broker's contract specifications before you trade.
